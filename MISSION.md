## 1. Executive Summary & Emotional Vision

On **25 October 2026**, Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer (תמר שפר) reaches her 70th birthday. 

This project is a dedicated, loving, and intellectually rigorous gift created by her family to celebrate her extraordinary life journey. Rather than a conventional digital photo album or a generic slideshow, this project builds a permanent, living archive and an atmospheric spatial journey that intertwines her lived biography—her parents' warm home in Herzliya, her decades building a family across Haifa, her travels with her husband Gadi, her bond with her late sister Er'ela, and her delight in her granddaughters—with the visceral, uncompromising universe of her installation art.

The emotional core of this project rests on **absolute authenticity**. It rejects superficial tributes, corporate templates, and hollow 3D gimmicks. Every narrative anchor, photograph, material texture, and spatial perspective must reflect Tami's true voice: her keen intellect, dry wit, fierce integrity, systemic rigor, and deep, quiet devotion to those she loves.

## 2. The Dual Identity: The Systems Engineer & The Tactile Artist

Tami's life is defined by a rare and profound synthesis of two seemingly disparate worlds: the analytical rigor of high-tech defense systems engineering and the raw, vulnerable tactility of contemporary installation art.

### 2.1 The Systems Engineer and Workplace Feminist
Graduating with a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the Technion—where she met her husband Gadi—Tami chose the practical path and dedicated decades of her career to Israel's defense tech giants: **Elbit Systems** and **Rafael Advanced Defense Systems**. Navigating a deeply patriarchal, male-dominated engineering culture, she proved herself through sheer technical brilliance, architectural vision, and unyielding professionalism. 

She did not merely succeed within the system; she fought to reform it. As an outspoken workplace feminist, Tami consistently challenged structural inequities, mentored younger women engineers, and advocated for dignified, equitable professional treatment long before such stances became fashionable. This period forged her acute understanding of complex systems, structural dependencies, and the interplay between human operators and rigid machines.

### 2.2 The Tactile Installation Artist
Following early retirement from Rafael, Tami embarked on a second, deeply courageous creative life. Immersing herself in rigorous study at **HaMidrasha School of Art (המדרשה - בית ברל)** (2005–2009) and earning her MFA at the **University of Haifa** (2009–2011), she quickly established herself as a significant voice in Israeli contemporary art:
- **Binyamin Gallery (גלריה בנימין):** As a founding cooperative member of this pioneering artist-run space in Tel Aviv, she helped sustain a vibrant community of independent artistic solidarity.
- **Cité Internationale des Arts (Paris, 2016):** A formative 6-month residency alongside Gadi, dwelling in a studio overlooking the Seine and Île Saint-Louis, immersing herself in European architectural history (Maison de Verre, Le Corbusier) and refining her drawing practice.
- **Bogliasco Foundation Fellow (Italy, 2020) & The LAB (Tel Aviv, 2019):** Developing major conceptual installations exploring time, enclosure, and Mediterranean light.
- **Ramat Gan Museum of Israeli Art ("בנה ביתך / The New Normal", 2026):** Her landmark solo exhibition, curated by Sari Golan, serving as a powerful cultural milestone during her 70th year, interrogating the disintegration of the Israeli domestic sanctuary and the post-traumatic reality of "home".

## 3. Artistic Philosophy & Tactile Materiality: The Non-Hollow Ethos

### 3.1 Material Truth
Tami's artistic language is fundamentally physical, low-tech, manual, and Sisyphean. She works in deliberate opposition to slick, sanitized digital aesthetics:
- **Paper & Papier-Mâché:** Brown packing paper, paper pulp, shredded municipal documents, and delicate paper skins molded over wire armatures.
- **Cement, Sand & Hamra Earth:** Heavy, brutalist building materials cast into fragile blocks, like the 200 hamra earth bricks pressed into QR-code patterns on tables in *"צופן / Code"* (Tel Aviv Museum, 2011).
- **Architectural Fragments & Reclaimed Domesticity:** Sawed chairs, severed table legs, conduit pipes, metal fittings, and disassembled closets (*"מרכז העיר פינת לב הבית"*).
- **Distorted Mirrors & Warped Reflection:** Mirrors coated in dust, scratched glass, and curved reflective surfaces that refract rather than reproduce reality.

### 3.2 The Conceptual Core: Viewpoint-Dependent Reality & "The Home"
In Tami's own words:
> *"My work deals with mental environments, imaginary architectures that lack structural logic, and the 'capsule'—a section of enclosed space and time... reality is viewpoint-dependent."*

Her work channels a profound thematic tension: the anxiety that **"the home" (הבית)**—both the private domestic sanctuary and the collective Israeli civic reality—is precarious, ungrounded, and threatened from within and without. This architectural unease directly mirrors her inner life: her deep concern for Israel's democratic soul and political crisis, her acute awareness of vulnerability, and her fierce protective instinct toward her family's health and safety.

### 3.3 The Digital "Non-Hollow" Mandate
In translating Tami's world into an interactive medium, this project enforces an absolute design standard:
1. **No Hollow Boxes:** We reject generic 3D polygon cubes, low-poly wireframes, or hollow camera tours that feel like video game levels.
2. **Layered Depth with Background Occlusion Synthesis:** Every 2.5D spatial scene constructed from archival photographs must feature synthesized background plates (via AI Fourier inpainting). When the camera shifts perspective, it must reveal genuine hidden space behind figures and sculptures, not black holes or rubber-sheet tearing.
3. **Tactile Atmospheric Layers:** Interactive surfaces must incorporate high-resolution scans of paper fibers, concrete grain, and dust motes, preserving the tactile intimacy of her physical studio.

## 4. Archival Longevity & Delivery Architecture

This project is built to endure. A birthday gift that breaks two years later due to an abandoned cloud subscription or deprecated JavaScript framework is a failure of craftsmanship.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    THE DUAL-RELEASE ARCHITECTURE                                        |
+---------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+
|            TRACK 1: RESPONSIVE WEB APP / PWA      |         TRACK 2: ARCHIVAL TIME-CAPSULE BUNDLE       |
|  - Family preview link prior to 25/10/2026        |  - Physical engraved USB-C gift drive               |
|  - Seamless on iOS Safari, iPadOS, Desktop Chrome |  - 100% offline, self-contained static directory    |
|  - Client-side Zero-Knowledge AES-256 Privacy Gate|  - Double-click launchers (macOS / Windows)         |
|  - PWA Service Worker offline caching             |  - Single-file standalone HTML fallback             |
|  - Zero public indexing by search engines         |  - Guaranteed operational survival for 20+ years    |
+---------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+
|                                          CORE GUARANTEE:                                                |
|  ZERO dynamic server dependencies. ZERO paid SaaS runtimes. ZERO external database rot.                |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 4.1 Track 1: Responsive Web App / PWA (Family Celebration Delivery)
- Delivered as an ultra-fast, responsive web application usable across desktop displays (1440x900+) and mobile devices (iPhone, iPad, Android).
- **Zero-Knowledge Family Privacy Gate:** To safeguard family intimacy without requiring annoying username/password accounts, the app uses in-memory AES-256-GCM client-side decryption. The entry screen presents a warm family prompt in Hebrew (e.g., asking for the name of the street in Denya or a beloved family date). Correct entry instantly decrypts the image catalog and media paths in browser RAM. No crawler, bot, or unauthorized visitor can view private family photographs.
- PWA manifest and Service Worker enable one-touch installation on Tami's iPad or phone, caching the complete experience for offline enjoyment.

### 4.2 Track 2: Archival Time-Capsule Edition (Physical Master Gift)
- Presented at the 70th birthday celebration on a custom-engraved wooden USB-C flash drive.
- Contains the complete offline repository:
  - Clean HTML5, CSS3, ES Modules, and Three.js WebGL engine with local vendor libraries.
  - Native double-click launchers: `start_mac.command` (macOS) and `start_win.bat` (Windows).
  - Standalone single-file HTML fallback (`tami70_standalone.html`) with embedded base64 assets for instant viewing on any computer without running a local web server.
- Built to standard web specifications that will remain viewable in 2046 and beyond.

## 5. Strict Read-Only Safety & Surprise Preservation

Because this project is an unrevealed surprise gift for Tami, data harvesting and development must adhere to unbreakable operational guardrails:

1. **100% Read-Only Personal Data Access:**
   - Any local inspection of WhatsApp archives (`~/Library/Group Containers/group.net.whatsapp.WhatsApp.shared` or `/Users/talsheaffer/compost-bot/auth`) or local browser profiles must be completely non-mutating.
   - **ZERO outbound messages, poll creations, status updates, or WebSocket pings** may ever be initiated.
   - Any database inspection must operate on read-only handles or isolated working copies.
2. **Surprise Preservation:**
   - No external communications or notifications that could alert Tami, her gallery peers, or family members before the scheduled unveiling on 25 October 2026.
3. **Data Integrity & Non-Fabrication:**
   - Every biographical assertion must link to verifiable source provenance (father's blog, bookified PDFs, official artist website, museum catalog, or user seed).
   - Any missing or uncertain dates/memories (e.g. details regarding sister Er'ela or friends Beni & Peri) must be transparently tagged with `[Verification needed for family]` rather than hallucinated.
