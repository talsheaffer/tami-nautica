# Solo Exhibition: "בנה ביתך / The New Normal"
# תערוכת יחיד: "בנה ביתך" (מוזיאון רמת גן לאמנות ישראלית, 2026)

From **April 30 to December 12, 2026**, coinciding precisely with the milestone of her 70th birthday, Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer presents her major institutional solo exhibition, **"בנה ביתך / The New Normal" (Build Your Home / The New Normal)**, at the **Ramat Gan Museum of Israeli Art (מוזיאון רמת גן לאמנות ישראלית)**[^rgma-bene-beitcha].

Curated by **Sari Golan (שרי גולן)** with assistant curator Gili Zeidman, the exhibition is part of the museum's flagship cluster *"The Local: Multiculturalism in the Israeli Art Field"*. It stands as the crowning critical and aesthetic achievement of Tami's artistic career, confronting the post-traumatic collapse of the Israeli domestic sanctuary in the wake of war, political division, and existential crisis[^prtfl-bene-beitcha].

---

## Conceptual Core: The Deconstructed Home

The title of the exhibition, *"בנה ביתך"* ("Build Your Home"), invokes the iconic Israeli civil slogan of the 1970s and 1980s that encouraged citizens to build private, detached single-family houses across the country as a symbol of domestic tranquility and middle-class stability. In Tami's installation, however, this bourgeois promise of safety is laid bare and utterly dismantled:

<div dir="rtl" lang="he">
> "התערוכה 'בנה ביתך' עוסקת במושג הבית שהתערער וקרס. הגבול המגן בין המרחב הפרטי האינטימי לבין המרחב הציבורי האלים נפרץ לחלוטין. הבית אינו עוד מבצר אלא מקום שביר, זמני, המורכב מסימולקרות, תחליפים ושרידים."
</div>

> *"The exhibition 'Build Your Home' deals with the concept of home that has been destabilized and collapsed. The protective boundary between the intimate private space and the violent public sphere has been completely breached. The home is no longer a fortress, but a fragile, temporary locus composed of simulacra, stand-ins, and remnants."*

---

## Key Artworks & Spatial Flow

The exhibition fills the museum gallery with large-scale architectural and sculptural interventions executed in Tami's signature low-tech, Sisyphean materiality:

### 1. *עקורים* ("Uprooted")
Positioned at the threshold of the exhibition, this haunting introductory installation consists of sculptural fragments resembling teeth ripped out together with their roots, or battered tent poles severed from their moorings. The work sets an immediate tone of displacement and visceral shock, evoking the hundreds of thousands of evacuated and uprooted Israeli citizens living in temporary hotel rooms across the country following October 7, 2023.

### 2. *המפטי-דמפטי* ("Humpty Dumpty")
A monumental sculptural hybrid combining shattered domestic furniture—table legs, cabinet carcasses, fractured chairs—surgically bisected and reassembled using industrial clamps, mortar, and wire. The nursery rhyme reference underlines the tragic impossibility of putting broken domestic structures back together again after a shattering impact.

### 3. *מרכז העיר פינת לב הבית* ("Downtown Corner Heart of the Home")
Expanding upon her acclaimed 2023 presentation at the Jerusalem Artists' Studios, this installation features domestic wardrobes and partitions that have turned inside out. Intimate household relics—notebooks, folded garments, Advil pill cookies (עוגיית אדויל), and small survival kits—stand exposed to the clinical gaze of the public sphere[^erev-rav-beitcha].

---

## Critical Reception & Significance for the 70th Milestone

Reviewing the exhibition in *Portfolio Magazine*, critics hailed Tami's ability to capture the specific psychological frequency of contemporary Israeli life—the pervasive anxiety that "the ground beneath our feet is giving way"—without resorting to superficial sensationalism or cliché political slogans[^prtfl-bene-beitcha]:
- Rather than displaying literal ruins, Tami exposes the psychological architecture of trauma through the meticulous, quiet craft of wood, paper, and concrete.
- The exhibition represents the absolute synthesis of Tami's life: the defense systems engineer who understands ballistic impacts and structural mechanics, merged with the deeply sensitive mother, grandmother, and citizen mourning the loss of national innocence.

For Tami at 70, *"בנה ביתך"* is both a critical pinnacle and a profound act of civic witness.
