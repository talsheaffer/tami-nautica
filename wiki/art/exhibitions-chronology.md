# Chronological Catalog of Exhibitions
# קטלוג כרונולוגי של תערוכות

This document provides the complete, authoritative exhibition chronology of Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer from her graduation from HaMidrasha in 2009 through her landmark 70th-birthday solo museum exhibition in 2026. Every entry is annotated with exhibition titles, venues, curators, dates, and exhibited works[^site-cv].

---

## 1. Solo & Institutional Exhibitions (תערוכות יחיד)

| Year | Exhibition Title | Venue | Curator(s) | Primary Artworks & Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2026** | **"בנה ביתך / The New Normal"** | **Ramat Gan Museum of Israeli Art** | **Sari Golan** (assistant: Gili Zeidman) | Major museum solo exhibition celebrating 70th milestone. Features *עקורים*, *המפטי-דמפטי*, *מרכז העיר פינת לב הבית*. |
| **2023** | **"מרכז העיר פינת לב הבית"** | **Jerusalem Artists' Studios** (סדנאות האמנים בירושלים) | **Dr. Irit Carmon Popper** | Deconstructed domestic wardrobe partition, *עוגיית אדויל*, *ערכת הישרדות*. |
| **2022** | **"כאן גרה אשה שחיפשה את עצמה ומצאה אהבה"** | **Public Space, Haifa** (*"שתי נקודות דמיוניות, והנוף"*) | **Tse'ela Kotler Hadari** | Site-specific permanent installation in Haifa public realm honoring women's memory amidst shikun wall ruins. |
| **2021** | **"להזמין את החושך"** (Inviting Darkness) | **Binyamin Gallery**, Tel Aviv | **Anat Getanio** | Solo exhibition featuring *מרחב לא מוגן* (hybrid wardrobe/Transformer), domestic assemblages. |
| **2020** | **"Flight Mode"** | **Rehovot Municipal Gallery** | **Ora Kraus** | Installation exploring detachment, airspace, and spatial lockdown. |
| **2019** | **"Low Sun"** (שמש נמוכה) | **The LAB**, Tel Aviv | **Sharon Toval** | Site-specific residency exhibition exploring Mediterranean light, shadow, and architectural conduits. |
| **2015** | **"Holding On"** | **Artists' House**, Tel Aviv | **Irena Gordon** | Large-scale paper and armature installations examining structural suspension. |
| **2013** | **"now \ (here+there)"** | **Binyamin Gallery**, Tel Aviv | **Tali Tamir** | Solo debut at Binyamin Gallery; layered drawings and dislocated architectural interventions. |
| **2012** | **"Indication for a Place"** | **The Gallery at Kiryat HaOmanim**, Kiryat Tivon | **Yifat Ben Natan** | Conceptual mapping and material place indicators. |
| **2012** | **"The Possibility of a Site"** | **Janco-Dada Museum**, Ein Hod | **Raya Zomer** | Installation exploring constructive absurdism in dialogue with Dadaist legacy. |

## 2. Duo & Two-Person Exhibitions (תערוכות זוגיות)

| Year | Exhibition Title | Collaborative Partner / Venue | Curator(s) | Key Artworks & Concept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2018** | **"Plan B"** | **Alfred Institute for Contemporary Art**, Tel Aviv | **Ravit Harari** | Site-specific conduit labyrinth, cardboard, cement, bulbs, Etgar Keret's *Pipes*, beach matkot soundtrack. |
| **2017** | **"NoEnd"** | **Givat Haim Ihud Gallery** | **Ruti Chinsky-Amitay & Hanush Morag** | Dialogue on infinite architectural horizons and spatial loops. |
| **2009** | **"Connections"** (חיבורים) | **City Gallery of Kfar Saba** | **Revital Segev** | Graduation presentation marking formal transition from engineering to fine art. |

## 3. Special Projects & Prestigious Residencies

| Year | Project / Residency | Location / Institution | Significance & Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2020** | **Bogliasco Foundation Fellow** | **Bogliasco Study Center**, Genoa, Liguria, Italy | Prestigious residential fellowship; research on Mediterranean coastal light and sculptural enclosures. |
| **2019** | **The LAB Residency** | **The LAB**, Tel Aviv | Experimental studio residency leading directly into solo exhibition *"Low Sun"*. |
| **2016** | **Cité Internationale des Arts Residency** | **Paris, France** (Studio 8422, Le Marais) | 6-month residency selected by Israel Ministry of Culture; deep study of European architecture and drafting. |
| **2014** | **Fresh Paint 7 (צבע טרי 7)** | Artists' Greenhouse, Tel Aviv | Featured artist in national contemporary art fair greenhouse. |
| **2012** | **"Code" (צופן) at 8-Cube** | **Tel Aviv Museum of Art** | Curators: Nili Goren z"l, Sally Haftel Naveh, Nogah Davidson. 200 pressed hamra earth blocks. |

## 4. Selected Group Exhibitions (תערוכות קבוצתיות נבחרות)

- **2024:** *"שטיחים"* (Carpets), The L.A. Mayer Museum for Islamic Art, Jerusalem. Curators: Idit Sharoni & Naama Brosh. Exhibited: *הגלגול הבא* (Next Reincarnation — rolled carpet containing home earth).
- **2019:** *"Prom"*, Beit Tami, Tel Aviv. Curator: Carmit Blumensohn.
- **2018:** *"It Was Me"*, Zuzu Gallery. Curator: Rotem Ritov.
- **2017:** *"The 3rd Israeli Paper Biennale"*, Eretz Israel Museum, Tel Aviv. Curator: Anat Getanio. Exhibited: *אולי שם יהיה יותר טוב / It Might Be Better There*.
- **2017:** *"Imaginary Landscapes"*, WIZO Haifa Academy of Design. Curator: Anat Getanio.
- **2016:** *"Reshamim 6"* (Traces 6 — Israel Drawing Biennale), Jerusalem. Curators: Sally Haftel Naveh & Edna Moshenson.
- **2016:** *"Ba Sade Ha Samuch"*, Studio Dieci, Vercelli, Italy. Curators: Diego Pasqualin & Dario Arkel.
- **2016:** *"A Hundred Years of DADA"*, Janco-Dada Museum, Ein Hod. Curator: Raya Zomer.
- **2015:** *"Local Pulse 2 \ Mix & Match"*, Artists' House, Tel Aviv. Curators: Orly Hoffman & Arie Berkovitch.
- **2015:** *"Out of Proportions"*, Open University Gallery, Ra'anana. Curator: Carmit Blumensohn.
- **2014:** *"Partners"*, Chelouche Gallery, Tel Aviv. Curator: Tal Amitai-Lavi.
- **2014:** *"Disturbed Landscape"*, Binyamin Gallery, Tel Aviv. Curator: Shahar Markus.
- **2013:** *"Urban Nature"* (Reshamim V — Drawing Biennale), Jerusalem. Curator: Tal Yahas.
- **2013:** *"Not Book"*, Beit Ha'ir, Tel Aviv. Curator: Dr. Guy Morag.
- **2013:** *"Concrete Abstract"*, BAAD Gallery (Bezalel), Tel Aviv. Curator: Ricardo Roistaczer.
- **2013:** *"The Eye of the Blackbird"*, Artists' House, Tel Aviv. Curator: Irena Gordon.
- **2011:** *"Certified Copy"*, The Holiday of Holidays (חג החגים), Haifa. Curator: Ye'ala Hazut.
