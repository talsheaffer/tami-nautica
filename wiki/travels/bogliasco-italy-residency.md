# Bogliasco Italy Residency: Ligurian Fellowship
# שהות אמנית בבוגליאסקו, איטליה

In **2020**, Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer was awarded a prestigious international fellowship at the **Bogliasco Foundation (Centro Studi Ligure per le Arti e le Lettere)**, situated in the coastal village of Bogliasco near Genoa, in the Liguria region of northwest Italy[^bogliasco-cv].

---

## The Bogliasco Foundation & Fellowship

The Bogliasco Foundation is one of the world's preeminent residential fellowships dedicated to the arts and humanities. Perched on dramatic terraced cliffs overlooking the Golfo Paradiso and the Ligurian Sea, the Bogliasco Study Center awards residential fellowships to an elite, highly competitive cohort of artists, writers, composers, and scholars from across the globe:
- **Interdisciplinary Community:** Fellowships bring together approximately 8 to 10 fellows at a time, creating an intimate, cloistered community of intellectuals and creators who live, dine, and converse together in historic villas surrounded by Mediterranean olive groves, pine forests, and citrus gardens.
- **Dedicated Studios:** Each visual arts fellow is granted a private, spacious working studio designed to maximize natural Mediterranean daylight and acoustic quiet.

---

## Artistic Research & Coastal Light

Tami's fellowship at Bogliasco was dedicated to deep research on the relationship between Mediterranean coastal topography, optical perspective, and sculptural shelter:
- **The Ligurian Coastline:** Bogliasco's dramatic cliffs—steep stone slopes dropping directly into crashing sea spray, connected by narrow stone tunnels, viaducts, and hillside paths—strongly echoed Tami's home landscape on Mount Carmel in Haifa.
- **Light & Enclosure:** In her Bogliasco studio, Tami experimented with new iterations of her sculptural capsules, studying how bright, raking Mediterranean sea light penetrates small perforations in paper and cement armatures.
- **Preparation for "Flight Mode" & "Inviting Darkness":** The contemplative weeks in Bogliasco laid the direct conceptual groundwork for her 2020 solo exhibition *"Flight Mode"* at the Rehovot Municipal Gallery and her 2021 solo exhibition *"להזמין את החושך" (Inviting Darkness)* at Binyamin Gallery in Tel Aviv.

---

## The Early 2020 Context

Tami's fellowship in Bogliasco coincided with the opening months of **2020**, a period that soon witnessed the unprecedented global outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic, with Northern Italy at its epicenter. The growing atmosphere of medical quarantine, border closures, and societal isolation uncannily mirrored the very themes Tami had explored in her art for years: the "capsule" as a sealed compartment of space/time, the breakdown of public circulation, and the fragile search for domestic sanctuary.

The Bogliasco fellowship cemented Tami's standing as an internationally recognized installation artist whose work addresses universal human vulnerabilities.
