## The Human Constellation | מערך המעגלים האנושיים

## Sub-Articles in this Portal | מאמרים בשער המשפחה

1. **[Parents: Ahuva & Michael Fried](ahuva-and-michael-fried.md)**  
   The foundational generation: Ahuva (née Lubetkin, sister of Warsaw Ghetto Uprising hero Zivia Lubetkin) and her Herzliya kindergarten; Michael Fried (illegal immigrant on the *Parita* in 1939), his "hands of gold" (ידי זהב), his safety worries, and his devotion to *The Bold and the Beautiful*.
2. **[Sister: Er'ela Fried](erela-fried.md)**  
   The irreplaceable bond of sisterhood: growing up together in Herzliya, daily phone calls filled with unstoppable, infectious laughter, a shared creative sensibility, and enduring memory.
3. **[Husband & Partner: Gad "Gadi" Sheaffer](gad-sheaffer.md)**  
   Five decades of marriage and partnership: meeting at the Technion, raising a family across Haifa, Gadi’s woodworking craft, the family chronicle blog *דרכי עצים*, his affectionate naming of Tami as *"זוגתי שתחיה"*, and their shared international travels.
4. **[Sons: Guy & Tal Sheaffer](sons-guy-and-tal.md)**  
   Generational pride: eldest son Guy (born during the Arlozorov 87 era, father of granddaughters, travel companion in Provence) and younger son Tal (born during the Einstein St. era, living in Montreal and NYC, initiator and custodian of this 70th-birthday gift).
5. **[Granddaughters: Ya'ara & Carmel](granddaughters-yaara-carmel.md)**  
   Radiant joy and play: Guy's daughters, Ya'ara (K-pop dances, creativity) and Carmel (playground swings, wild boars), bringing laughter and light to Savta Tami on the carpet in Denya.
6. **[Close Friends: Beni & Peri](close-friends-beni-peri.md)**  
   Lifelong comradeship: loyal friends who have shared decades of milestone celebrations, kitchen-table conversations, and steadfast companionship.
