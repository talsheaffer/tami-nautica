## The Human Constellation | מערך המעגלים האנושיים

While Tami Sheaffer’s professional and [artistic achievements](../art/index.md) are formidable, the ultimate anchor of her life has always been her family[^bio-master]. From the sunlit [kindergarten yard of Herzliya](../places/herzliya-home-and-yard.md) to the Carmel slopes of [Haifa](../places/haifa-greenboim-80-denya.md), her world is defined by bonds of quiet devotion, dry humor, and mutual loyalty[^blog-ahuva][^blog-michael].

## Sub-Articles in this Portal | מאמרים בשער המשפחה

1. **[Parents: Ahuva & Michael Fried](ahuva-and-michael-fried.md)**  
   The foundational generation: Ahuva (née Lubetkin, sister of Warsaw Ghetto Uprising leader Zivia Lubetkin, *״מלח הארץ״* and beloved Herzliya kindergarten teacher)[^blog-ahuva] and Michael Fried (1939 *Parita* *מעפיל*, British Army driver of the truck named *״אהובה״*, and master craftsman of *״ידי זהב״*)[^blog-michael].
2. **[Sister: Er'ela Sadan](erela-sadan.md)**  
   The irreplaceable bond of sisterhood: growing up together in Herzliya, daily phone calls filled with unstoppable, infectious laughter, a shared creative sensibility, and enduring memory[^bio-master].
3. **[Husband & Partner: Gad "Gadi" Sheaffer](gad-sheaffer.md)**  
   Five decades of marriage and partnership: meeting in Linear Algebra 1 at the Technion[^blog-algebra], raising a family across Haifa, Gadi’s woodworking craft, the family chronicle blog *[דרכי עצים](https://theshingimel.wordpress.com/)*[^blog-root], and their shared international journeys.
4. **[Sons: Guy & Tal Sheaffer](sons-guy-and-tal.md)**  
   Generational continuity: eldest son Guy (born during the [Arlozorov 87](../places/haifa-arlozorov-87.md) era, father of Ya'ara and Carmel, surprise travel companion in [Marseille](../travels/southern-france-and-paris.md)) and younger son Tal (raised on [Einstein St.](../places/haifa-einstein.md), living in [Montreal and New York](../travels/montreal-and-new-york.md), initiator and custodian of this 70th-birthday tribute)[^blog-4star].
5. **[Granddaughters: Ya'ara & Carmel](granddaughters-yaara-carmel.md)**  
   Radiant joy and play: Guy's daughters, Ya'ara (K-pop birthday dances, drawing, and crafts)[^blog-kpop] and Carmel (*״כרמל, נכדתי המתוקה״*, playground swings and Haifa wild boars)[^blog-strawberry], bringing laughter and light to Savta Tami in Denya.
6. **[Close Friends: Beni & Peri](close-friends-beni-peri.md)**  
   Lifelong comradeship: loyal friends who have shared decades of milestone celebrations, kitchen-table conversations, and steadfast companionship.
