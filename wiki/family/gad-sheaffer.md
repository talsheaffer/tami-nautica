# Husband & Partner: Gad "Gadi" Sheaffer
# בן הזוג והשותף לחיים: גד (גדי) שפר

> **Subject:** Gad "Gadi" Sheaffer (גד שפר, born 1954)  
> **Core Dimensions:** Technion Partners • Woodworker & Chronicler • Author of *דרכי עצים* • "זוגתי שתחיה"

---

## 1. An Intellectual & Life Partnership | שותפות חיים אינטלקטואלית

For nearly fifty years, **Gad "Gadi" Sheaffer** has been Tami Sheaffer’s husband, confidant, intellectual foil, and steadfast companion[^seed-gadi]. Born in Haifa in 1954, Gadi studied engineering at the **Technion — Israel Institute of Technology**, where he and Tami met in the mid-1970s[^blog-about]. 

Their marriage has been a true egalitarian union. Together, they navigated the demands of engineering careers in Israel's defense tech ecosystem, designed and built three homes across Haifa, raised their two sons Guy and Tal, and embraced extensive travels around the globe.

---

## 2. The Voice of "דרכי עצים" & "זוגתי שתחיה" | קולו של הבלוג "דרכי עצים"

Gadi is the creator and author of the celebrated Hebrew family chronicle and woodworking blog, **"דרכי עצים" (*Darchei Etzim*)** (`theshingimel.wordpress.com`), maintained continuously from February 2011 through 2026 across more than 1,250 published essays[^blog-stats].

In his writing, Gadi consistently refers to Tami with enduring, tender irony as:
> *"זוגתי שתחיה"* (or the affectionate abbreviation *"זוג"ש"*) — "my wife, may she live long!"[^blog-usage]

Gadi's blog constitutes a monumental living archive of their family life. With a sharp eye for detail, literary elegance, and deep affection, Gadi has documented:
- Tami’s artistic exhibitions, studio setup, and residency preparations.
- Memorial tributes to Tami’s parents, Ahuva and Michael Fried.
- Family road trips through Provence, Montreal, New York, and Liguria.
- The growth, dances, and outdoor antics of their granddaughters Ya'ara and Carmel.

---

## 3. Woodworking Craft & Parallel Artistry | חרשות עץ ואמנות מקבילה

Gadi is himself an accomplished artisan. In his dedicated home workshop at 80 Greenboim St., Denya, he practices fine woodworking, creating handcrafted furniture, turned wooden bowls, intricate joinery, and functional design objects.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    A HOUSEHOLD OF TWO CRAFTSMEN                                         |
+---------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+
|            GADI SHEAFFER: WOODCRAFTSMAN           |           TAMI SHEAFFER: INSTALLATION ARTIST        |
| - Medium: Hardwoods, grain, precision joints      | - Medium: Paper pulp, packing paper, cast concrete  |
| - Ethos: Structural harmony, durability, utility  | - Ethos: Precarity, structural illogic, simulacra   |
| - Space: Workshop on Greenboim St.                | - Space: Art studio on Greenboim St.                |
| - Voice: "דרכי עצים" (Word & Grain)               | - Voice: Monumental Museum Installations            |
+---------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+
```

This mutual devotion to manual craft creates a harmonious dialogue within their home: both value material truth, the honest labor of the hands, and the patience required to transform raw matter into form.

## 4. Shared Travels & The Paris Residency | מסעות משותפים ושהות בפריז

Gadi has been Tami’s faithful companion on all her major artistic sojourns:
- **Paris Cité Residency (2016–2017):** Gadi lived with Tami for six months in her studio at the Cité Internationale des Arts, overlooking the Seine opposite Île Saint-Louis. He chronicled their daily walks, museum visits, encounters with architecture (Maison de Verre, Villa Savoye), and market discoveries[^pdf-paris].
- **North American Journeys:** Traveling repeatedly to Montreal and New York to visit Tal during his studies and career abroad[^seed-travels].
- **Southern France & Provence:** Journeying through Arles and Provence alongside Guy, visiting architectural monuments and Mediterranean landscapes[^seed-travels].

*Read more: [Paris Cité Studio](../places/paris-cite-studio.md) and [Southern France & Paris](../travels/southern-france-and-paris.md).*
