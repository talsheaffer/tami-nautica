## 1. The Radiant Light of Savta Tami | האור של סבתא תמי

If Tami’s artistic studio is a realm of Sisyphean labor, existential inquiry, and heavy concrete, her time with her granddaughters **Ya'ara (יערה)** and **Carmel (כרמל)** is pure, unadulterated joy[^seed-granddaughters]. 

Daughters of her eldest son Guy, Ya'ara and Carmel bring out a tender, radiant playfulness in Tami. In their presence, the intense focus of the artist dissolves into laughter, games on the living room carpet in Denya, storytelling, and imaginative play.

## 2. Ya'ara (יערה): The Creative Spark & Dance | יערה: תנועה, קצב ויצירה

Ya'ara, the elder sister, is endowed with vibrant energy, keen curiosity, and a passion for rhythm and dance.

Her grandfather Gadi chronicled one of her standout milestones in *דרכי עצים*: her **7th birthday celebration**, where Ya'ara thrilled her family with an intricate, energetic K-pop dance performance that she had rehearsed with immense dedication[^blog-yaara]. Ya'ara also loves drawing and making crafts with Savta Tami, displaying the same sharp observational eye that runs through generations of the family.

---

## 3. Carmel (כרמל): Sweetness & Outdoor Adventures | כרמל: מתיקות והרפתקאות בחיפה

Carmel, the younger granddaughter, is documented with endless affection throughout Gadi’s essays under the loving title *"כרמל נכדתי המתוקה"* (Carmel, my sweet granddaughter)[^blog-carmel].

Carmel's visits to Saba and Savta's home in Denya are filled with outdoor joy:
- **The Playground Swings:** Soaring on the swings in neighborhood parks overlooking Haifa’s green wadis.
- **Encountering Haifa’s Wild Boars:** Wide-eyed, amusing sightings of Haifa's famous wild boars wandering peacefully along the Carmel ridges and suburban streets—an iconic local Haifa experience shared with her grandparents[^blog-carmel].

---

## 4. Multi-Generational Echoes of Herzliya | הד רב-דורי מחצר הרצליה

Watching Tami interact with Ya'ara and Carmel carries deep emotional resonance for anyone who knew her mother, Ahuva Fried. Just as Ahuva created a haven of warmth and creative play in her Herzliya kindergarten yard, Tami now provides that same generous, unhurried sanctuary for her granddaughters in Denya. It is a multi-generational lineage of love, patience, and creative nurturing.
