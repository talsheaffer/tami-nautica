## Welcome | פתח דבר

Welcome to the central knowledge repository and biographical graph dedicated to **Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer (תמר שפר)**. Created by her family on the occasion of her 70th birthday, this wiki documents the rich, interwoven tapestry of her life: her childhood in Herzliya, her long career as a defense systems engineer and outspoken workplace feminist, her deep partnership with her husband Gadi, the raising of their sons across Haifa, the joy of her granddaughters, and her prolific second act as an installation artist whose tactile work interrogates the precarious nature of human structures and "the home."

This wiki is organized into **five thematic portals** comprising **31 interconnected articles**, rigorously source-cited, bilingual in Hebrew and English, and free of hollow exaggerations.
