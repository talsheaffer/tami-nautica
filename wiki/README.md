# Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer: Biographical & Artistic Wiki
# ויקי ביוגרפי ואמנותי: תמר (תמי) שפר

> **Milestone Celebration:** 70th Birthday — 25 October 2026 (כ"ה בתשרי תשפ"ז)  
> **Project Subject:** [Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer (תמר שפר)](bio/tamar-sheaffer.md)  
> **Curatorial Custodians:** [Tal Sheaffer, Guy Sheaffer](family/sons-guy-and-tal.md), [Gad Sheaffer](family/gad-sheaffer.md), and [Family](family/index.md)

> ״עבודותי עוסקות במצבים שבריריים ולא יציבים, של חוסר בהירות ואי ודאות ביחס למציאות שמסביבנו, ובתחושות הבדידות והניכור הקיומי הנלוות למצבים אלו. האוביקטים והמיצבים שאני מציגה משלבים פיסול בחומרים ״דלים״ (עיסת נייר, צמנט ועוד), חפצי רדי-מייד ורישום. השפה בה אני משתמשת היא בעלת אופי ״ביישני״ הנובע גם מאופי הטיפול בחומר שלמרות היותו עמלני וסיזיפי שומר על איפוק ואלמנט של ארעיות.״
> — תמר שפר, מתוך הצהרת האמנית הרשמית, [עמוד האמנית בגלריה בנימין](https://www.binyamin-gallery.com/artist/tamar-sheaffer)[^binyamin-statement]

## Welcome | פתח דבר

Welcome to the central knowledge repository and biographical graph dedicated to **[Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer (תמר שפר)](bio/tamar-sheaffer.md)**[^tamar-site][^binyamin-statement]. Created by her family on the occasion of her 70th birthday, this wiki documents the interwoven chapters of her life: her childhood in [Herzliya](places/herzliya-home-and-yard.md)[^blog-ahuva], her decades-long first career as a [systems engineer and workplace feminist](bio/engineering-and-feminism.md)[^prtfl-interview], her fifty-year partnership with [Gadi](family/gad-sheaffer.md)[^blog-root], raising [Guy and Tal](family/sons-guy-and-tal.md) across [Haifa](places/index.md), the radiant joy of granddaughters [Ya'ara and Carmel](family/granddaughters-yaara-carmel.md)[^blog-kpop], and her acclaimed second career as an installation artist culminating in *[בנה ביתך / The New Normal](art/solo-exhibition-bene-beitcha.md)* at the Ramat Gan Museum of Israeli Art (2026)[^prtfl-bene].

This wiki is organized into **five thematic portals** comprising **31 interconnected articles**, with all primary quotes preserved verbatim in their original language (Hebrew or English) and linked directly to clickable external and internal sources.

### 2. [Family & Close Relationships | משפחה ומעגלי קִרבה](family/index.md)
The warm human foundations that anchor Tami's world:

- [Er'ela Sadan](family/erela-sadan.md) — Sister: lifelong sisterhood, daily phone laughter, shared creative sensibility, and cherished memory.
- [Gad "Gadi" Sheaffer](family/gad-sheaffer.md) — Husband, Technion partner, woodworker, and author of *[דרכי עצים](https://theshingimel.wordpress.com/)*[^blog-root].
- [Sons: Guy & Tal Sheaffer](family/sons-guy-and-tal.md) — Eldest Guy (Arlozorov, father of Ya'ara & Carmel) & younger Tal (Einstein, Montreal/NYC, project custodian).
- [Granddaughters: Ya'ara & Carmel](family/granddaughters-yaara-carmel.md) — Pure joy, games on the carpet, K-pop birthday dances, playground swings, and Haifa wild boars[^blog-kpop].
- [Close Friends: Beni & Peri](family/close-friends-beni-peri.md) — Decades of shared loyalty, kitchen-table conversations, and lifelong comradeship.

### 3. [Lived Places & Sanctuaries | מרחבים, בתים וסטודיו](places/index.md)
The physical spaces that shaped Tami's memory and spatial imagination:

### 4. [Artistic Oeuvre & Practice | יצירה, חומרים ותערוכות](art/index.md)
The tactile, non-hollow universe of installation art:
- [Art Education: HaMidrasha & Haifa MFA](art/education-midrasha-haifa.md) — HaMidrasha Faculty of Arts (`2005–2009`) and University of Haifa MFA (`2009–2011`)[^tamar-site].
- [Artist Statement & Aesthetic Philosophy](art/artist-statement-philosophy.md) — Dissolving binaries, viewpoint-dependent truth, and *״שפה ביישנית״*[^binyamin-statement][^prtfl-interview].

- [Motifs: Capsules, Scaffolding & Distorted Mirrors](art/motifs-capsules-and-mirrors.md) — Hollow paper capsules, scaffolding conduits, and fractured reflections.
- [Binyamin Gallery Artists' Cooperative](art/binyamin-gallery-cooperative.md) — Collective member of Tel Aviv's cooperative gallery in Kiryat HaMelacha[^binyamin-statement].
- [Solo Exhibition: "בנה ביתך / The New Normal" (2026)](art/solo-exhibition-bene-beitcha.md) — Landmark museum solo exhibition at Ramat Gan Museum of Israeli Art, curated by Sari Golan[^prtfl-bene][^haaretz-bene].
