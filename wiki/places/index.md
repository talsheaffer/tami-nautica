# Lived Spaces & Geographic Sanctuaries
# מרחבי חיים ומשכנות

Welcome to the **Places Portal** of the Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer Biographical and Artistic Wiki.

For Tami, physical geography is never a passive backdrop; it is an active psychological and sculptural crucible. Every house, yard, and studio she has inhabited directly informs the architecture of her installations[^binyamin-artist][^blog-g80-index]:

> ״שתי חוויות מאוד שונות היו השראה לעבודה זו: סיפורו של בקט ״האבודים״, ו״בית״ שהיה לי בילדותי על עץ בחצר והיה מקום מפלט מושלם.״
> — תמר שפר על *אולי שם יהיה יותר טוב*, [עמוד האמנית בגלריה בנימין](https://www.binyamin-gallery.com/artist/tamar-sheaffer)[^binyamin-artist]

### 1. [Herzliya: Parents' Home & Kindergarten Yard](herzliya-home-and-yard.md)
The foundational sanctuary built with their own hands by **[Michael and Ahuva Fried](../family/ahuva-and-michael-fried.md)** (*שיכון חיילים משוחררים*)[^blog-michael]: Ahuva's warm ethos as *״הגננת אהובה״*, Michael's backyard wooden craft shed (*״ידי זהב״*), and Tami's childhood treehouse—later reborn in her sculptural capsules[^binyamin-artist].

### 2. [Haifa: 87 Arlozorov St. (Hadar HaCarmel)](haifa-arlozorov-87.md)
The young couple's urban beginning (`1982–1990`): a high-ceilinged rooftop corner flat at 87 Arlozorov / 1 Bar-Giora St. with skylight foyer, darkroom, handmade floor-to-ceiling bookcase, panoramic views to Lebanon, and the birth of eldest son **[Guy](../family/sons-guy-and-tal.md)**[^blog-arlozorov].

### 3. [Haifa: 33A Einstein St. (Ahuza)](haifa-einstein.md)
The quiet Carmel ridge haven (`1990–2004`): a rear building projecting into the lush public park between Einstein and Hantke streets (*״מוקף עצים מכל עבר ושקט כמו קבר״*), cooled by summer breezes beneath open *pilotis*, where [Guy and Tal](../family/sons-guy-and-tal.md) grew up alongside Didi the cat (*זחמ״ל*)[^blog-einstein].

### 4. [Haifa: 80 Greenboim St. (Denya — Home & Studio)](haifa-greenboim-80-denya.md)
The custom-built modernist sanctuary (`2004–present`): suspended above a wooded pocket of Nahal Tira, featured by Esther Zandberg in *Haaretz*[^haaretz-zandberg], shared nightly with Carmel porcupines and wild boars, and housing Tami's ground-floor art studio alongside Gadi's woodworking shop[^blog-g80].

### 5. [Paris: Studio 8422 at Cité Internationale des Arts](paris-cite-studio.md)
The European residency (`2016`): six months living and working in spartan Studio 8422 (`Israël`) on the Right Bank of the Seine in Le Marais[^blog-paris].

## Thematic Connections to Tami's Art | זיקות ליצירה

- **Enclosure & Refuge:** The childhood Herzliya treehouse evolved directly into her sculptural capsules ([Motifs: Capsules & Mirrors](../art/motifs-capsules-and-mirrors.md))[^binyamin-artist].
- **Literal Soil & Cement:** Soil gathered directly from the 80 Greenboim yard (*אדמה מחצר הבית*) coats paper spheres in works like *הגלגול הבא* ([Materials: Paper & Cement](../art/materials-paper-and-cement.md))[^binyamin-artist].
- **Domestic Fragility:** Building and sealing a home (*״בנה ביתך בתבונה״*) stands in direct dialogue with her 2026 museum solo exhibition **[״בנה ביתך / The New Normal״](../art/solo-exhibition-bene-beitcha.md)**[^bene-beitcha].
