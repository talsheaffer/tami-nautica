# Lived Spaces & Geographic Sanctuaries
# מרחבי חיים ומשכנות

Welcome to the **Places Portal** of the Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer Biographical and Artistic Wiki.

For Tami, physical geography is never a mere backdrop; it is an active psychological and sculptural crucible. The physical houses, yards, and studios she has inhabited throughout her life directly inform the architecture of her art. From the sunlit, fragrant kindergarten yard of her childhood in Herzliya to the historic heights of Hadar, the leafy tranquility of Ahuza, the custom-built concrete-and-glass sanctuary on Mount Carmel, and the monastic artist's cell overlooking the Seine in Paris, each place represents a distinct chapter in her emotional and creative evolution.

### 1. [Herzliya: Parents' Home & Kindergarten Yard](herzliya-home-and-yard.md)
The foundational sanctuary: the home of **Michael and Ahuva Fried** on the central coastal plain. A sprawling yard hosting Ahuva's beloved kindergarten and Michael's woodworking workshop with "hands of gold." Here, high in the branches of the backyard trees, Tami built her first childhood treehouse—a private refuge from the world that decades later became the direct inspiration for her celebrated paper capsule sculptures.

### 2. [Haifa: 87 Arlozorov St. (Hadar HaCarmel)](haifa-arlozorov-87.md)
The young family's urban beginning: the top-floor corner apartment at 87 Arlozorov / 1 Bar-Giora St. High ceilings, generous balconies, a rooftop darkroom, and a breathtaking panoramic view of Haifa Bay reaching into Lebanon. The home where Tami and Gadi began their married life and where eldest son **Guy** was born.

### 3. [Haifa: 33A Einstein St. (Ahuza)](haifa-einstein.md)
The leafy Carmel ridge haven: a tranquil rear apartment above stilt pillars overlooking the green ravine between Einstein and Hantke streets. The home where younger son **Tal** was born, where the boys grew up playing in the yard and park, and where the family sheltered during the 1991 Gulf War. It was the apartment Tami eventually outgrew when she left high-tech engineering to create art and needed space to build.

### 4. [Haifa: 80 Greenboim St. (Denya — Home & Studio)](haifa-greenboim-80-denya.md)
The custom-built architectural sanctuary: nestled in a lush wadi stream pocket in Hod HaCarmel (Denya). Designed with modernist principles, elevated on pillars for mountain breezes, and housing Tami's dedicated ground-floor art studio alongside Gadi's woodworking shop. The birthplace of her major installations, cement castings, and paper sculptures.

### 5. [Paris: Studio 8422 at Cité Internationale des Arts](paris-cite-studio.md)
The European artist's haven: a formative 6-month residency in 2016 in the 4th arrondissement (Le Marais). Living and working in Studio 8422 overlooking the Seine and Île Saint-Louis, immersing herself in modernist architecture, museum studies, and intense drafting.

## Thematic Connections to Tami's Art

The physical characteristics of these lived environments echo throughout Tami's artistic oeuvre:
- **Enclosure & Refuge:** The childhood Herzliya treehouse evolved directly into her signature sculptural "capsules" ([Motifs: Capsules & Mirrors](../art/motifs-capsules-and-mirrors.md)).
- **Raw Materiality:** The red hamra earth of Herzliya and the rocky limestone and brutalist concrete of Haifa's Carmel ridges form the material foundation of her sculptural practice ([Materials: Paper & Cement](../art/materials-paper-and-cement.md)).
- **Domestic Fragility:** The very act of designing, building, and maintaining these homes stands in sharp dialogue with her landmark exhibition interrogating the crumbling Israeli domestic sanctuary ([Solo Exhibition: "בנה ביתך"](../art/solo-exhibition-bene-beitcha.md)).
