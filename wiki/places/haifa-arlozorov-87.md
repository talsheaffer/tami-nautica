# Haifa: 87 Arlozorov St., Hadar
# ארלוזורוב 87, הדר הכרמל, חיפה

For a full decade from **1982 to 1992**, the top-floor apartment at **87 Arlozorov Street** (corner of 1 Bar-Giora Street) in Hadar HaCarmel served as the vibrant home where Tami and Gadi Sheaffer began their married life and entered parenthood[^blog-arlozorov].

---

## Architectural Character & History

The apartment was located on the topmost floor of an imposing, historic Bauhaus-era residential building originally constructed by the building's owner for his private residence[^blog-arlozorov]. This origin bestowed upon the space rare architectural grandeur:
- Soaring ceilings that created cavernous vertical volume.
- A generous entryway foyer illuminated by two overhead ceiling skylights.
- A private staircase ascending to a bathroom situated directly above the building's main stairwell ceiling, punctuated by a distinctive round porthole window.
- Expansive balconies wrapping the facade, opening northward to an unobstructed, breathtaking panorama spanning the entirety of Haifa Bay, Acre, and deep into the mountains of Lebanon.

The property had deep familial roots: Gadi's grandparents had lived there since the early 1950s. Following the grandfather's passing, Tami and Gadi moved into the apartment in **1982**[^blog-arlozorov]. It was the home to which they returned directly from their wedding ceremony, and the first property that was legally and emotionally their own.

---

## The Post-Graduation World Trip & Massive Renovation

For their first two years at Arlozorov 87, Tami and Gadi lived spartaneously without altering a thing—sleeping on a simple mattress on the floor of a side room[^blog-arlozorov]. During this initial chapter, they embarked on a transformative ten-month post-graduation journey around the world ("טיול של אחרי לימודים").

Upon their return, while pursuing graduate studies, they undertook a massive, daring architectural renovation. They completely reimagined the apartment's spatial flow:
- The formal, conservative "guest salon" was demolished and transformed into an expansive, light-filled open-plan kitchen and dining hall.
- The original cramped kitchen was converted into a nursery room (which three years later welcomed their firstborn son).
- In the shared study, Gadi built his very first major piece of custom woodworking: a monumental, floor-to-ceiling bookshelf spanning an entire wall.
- In an adjoining windowless storage closet, they installed a fully equipped photographic darkroom where Gadi developed black-and-white film and prints behind a door that doubled as a cabinet.

---

## The Birth of Guy & The Departure to the Ridge

At Arlozorov 87, Tami and Gadi became parents with the birth of their eldest son, **Guy Sheaffer (גיא שפר)**. Guy's infancy and early childhood unfolded in these sunlit, high-ceilinged rooms overlooking the sea.

However, as the Hadar neighborhood grew increasingly congested and noisy in the early 1990s, the constant roar of city traffic echoing into the third-floor apartment became exhausting. Seeking quiet, green surroundings, and a safe ground-level environment for raising small children, Tami and Gadi made the decision in 1992 to leave their beloved Hadar apartment and "ascend the mountain" to Ahuza[^blog-arlozorov].
