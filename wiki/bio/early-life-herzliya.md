## 1. The Herzliya Sanctuary | בית ההורים בהרצליה

Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer was born on October 25, 1956, and grew up in the coastal town of Herzliya in the house her parents built with their own hands in a discharged-soldiers' *shikun* (*שיכון חיילים משוחררים*), on land they had previously cultivated as a vegetable plot during their kibbutz years[^blog-ahuva][^blog-parita].

Her parents, **[Michael Fried and Ahuva Fried (née Lubetkin)](../family/ahuva-and-michael-fried.md)**, embodied the quiet resilience of a generation that survived catastrophe in Europe and built a modest, loving home from scratch:

> ״חוץ מזה שהיא ניפקה את זוגתי שתחיה, ועשתה זאת באמנות, היא הייתה (אני יודע, הביטוי נדוש ושחוק) – מלח הארץ. באמת. באמת מהאנשים שהכינו עבורנו את המקום הזה, במו ידיהם.״
> — גד שפר על חמותו אהובה פריד, [״אהובה״, בלוג *דרכי עצים* (8 ביוני 2013)](https://theshingimel.wordpress.com/2013/06/08/ahuva-fried/)[^blog-ahuva]

```
                                 [ THE HERZLIYA HOMESTEAD ]
                                              |
                     +------------------------+------------------------+
                     |                                                 |
                     v                                                 v
        [ AHUVA'S KINDERGARTEN & YARD ]                     [ MICHAEL'S WORKSHOP SHED ]
        - Warmth as lifelong educator                         - "Hands of gold" (ידי זהב)
        - Childhood treehouse sanctuary                       - Ceramics & silver jewelry
        - Generational care & hospitality                     - Security vigilance & Renaults
        - Former kibbutz vegetable plot                       - Afternoon *Bold & Beautiful*
```

### Ahuva's Warmth, Kindergarten Career & Yard Treehouse
Born in Byten (then Poland, today Belarus) on October 22, 1923, Ahuva came to Mandatory Palestine at age 14 in 1938 with Youth Aliyah to study agronomy at Ben Shemen—a departure that saved her life on the eve of the German invasion, in which all of her large family perished except her older sister Zivia Lubetkin and brother Shlomo[^blog-ahuva]. Working for most of her life as a kindergarten teacher (*גננת*), Ahuva radiated practical warmth; in the leafy yard of their Herzliya home stood Tami's beloved childhood treehouse—later immortalized in Tami's 2017 sculpture *אולי שם יהיה יותר טוב* (*״׳בית׳ שהיה לי בילדותי על עץ בחצר והיה מקום מפלט מושלם״*, [גלריה בנימין](https://www.binyamin-gallery.com/artist/tamar-sheaffer))[^binyamin-treehouse].

### Michael's "Hands of Gold" & Protective Vigilance
Born Paul ("Poli") Fried in Satu Mare, Romania on May 13, 1920, Michael landed on the beach of Tel Aviv on August 22, 1939 aboard the clandestine immigrant ship *Parita*, served in the British Army in Europe (painting *"אהובה"* in large letters on the side of his army truck), and later managed the vehicle fleet garage of Israel Military Industries (TAAS) in Ramat HaSharon[^blog-parita]. In his memorial tribute, Gadi described Michael's manual artistry:

> ״למיכאל היו ידי זהב, ממש, ותמיד היו לו תחביבים. הוא עסק בקרמיקה ובצורפות, בצריף קטן מעץ בירכתי החצר שלהם. שם הוא עשה יופי של תכשיטי כסף שהוא גם תכנן ועיצב, עד שראייתו התדרדרה והוא כבר לא היה מסוגל.״
> — גד שפר, [״מיכאל״, בלוג *דרכי עצים* (26 בנובמבר 2013)](https://theshingimel.wordpress.com/2013/11/26/fried-2/)[^blog-parita]

Family lore also cherishes Michael's tender anxiousness over his family's safety (*״טיפוס חרדתי״*), his devotion to Renault cars (at one point placing a Renault 12 driver's seat in front of the television), and his daily loyalty to the soap opera *The Bold and the Beautiful* (*היפים והאמיצים*)[^blog-parita][^seed-herzliya].

*Read more: [Parents: Ahuva & Michael Fried](../family/ahuva-and-michael-fried.md) and [Herzliya Home & Yard](../places/herzliya-home-and-yard.md).*

## 2. Sisterly Bond with Er'ela | הקשר עם אראלה

Growing up alongside her sister **[Er'ela](../family/erela-sadan.md)**, Tami experienced a bond of deep closeness, shared aesthetic sensitivity, and infectious humor[^seed-erela]. Even as adults living in different cities, their near-daily telephone conversations were famous in the household for peals of unstoppable laughter echoing down the corridor[^seed-erela].

*Read more: [Sister: Er'ela Sadan](../family/erela-sadan.md).*

### Cousin & Childhood Friend: Ada Wertheimer | בת הדודה וחברת הילדות: עדה ורטהיימר

According to the family, Tami's cousin **Ada Wertheimer (עדה ורטהיימר)** was also one of her closest childhood friends during the Herzliya years[^fb-ada].

**Archival photograph on the Herzliya porch (December 2006):** In the family media catalog (`IMG_HERZ_0388`, [צילום המרפסת בהרצליה — IMG_HERZ_0388](../../media/herzliya_fried_house/fried2_pict0056_70fbe50d.jpg)), Ada Wertheimer is photographed standing beside Tami (who sits at the porch table wrapped in a burgundy sweater) at the [Herzliya parents' home](../places/herzliya-home-and-yard.md) on December 2, 2006, six days after Michael Fried's passing[^fb-ada-photo]. Tal Sheaffer verified Ada's identity directly in family review, correcting an earlier catalog caption that had mistakenly attributed her portrait to Tami's sister [Er'ela Sadan](../family/erela-sadan.md)[^fb-ada-photo].

**What the public record confirms (via the family blog):** In his memorial post for Ahuva, [Gadi](../family/gad-sheaffer.md) writes that *״גם אחייניתם עדה כתבה עליהם, כתבה נפלא״* — "their niece Ada also wrote about them, beautifully" — and quotes her poem *״הזכרת נשמות״* (December 2006), written after [Ahuva and Michael](../family/ahuva-and-michael-fried.md) died within a few months of each other[^blog-ahuva]. So Ada is a niece of Tami's parents, which makes her Tami's first cousin. The poem remembers Michael *״צעדיו איטיים על שביל הכניסה״* (walking slowly up the entrance path) and both of them on the phone, *״דורשים לדעת, לפני הכל, מה שלומי״* (asking first of all how she was)[^iada-poem-1]. A follow-up poem shows Michael *״מנופף לשלום לבדו בקצה שביל הכניסה, שעון על מקלו״* (waving goodbye alone at the end of the entrance path, leaning on his cane)[^iada-poem-2]. This is the same entrance path of the [Herzliya house](../places/herzliya-home-and-yard.md) that appears in the December 2006 family photos. In 2013, Ada left warm comments on both of Gadi's memorial posts, for Ahuva and for Michael[^blog-ahuva-comment][^blog-michael-comment].

> ⚠️ **Uncertainty:** The blog that holds the poems is signed **"עדה ק."** (Ada K.). It is a backup of her Israblog posts from 2006–2014. No source we found prints the surname "Wertheimer" next to this blog, so we have **not** confirmed that "Ada K." and "Ada Wertheimer" are the same person (for example, a married name versus a maiden name). We also don't know which side of the family she comes from: the Lubetkin side (Ahuva) or the Fried side (Michael). A public web search also finds a Hebrew linguist, Dr. Ada Wertheimer (David Yellin College). No source connects her to the Fried family, so she should not be treated as Tami's cousin unless the family confirms it. While Ada's December 2006 porch photograph (`IMG_HERZ_0388`) is confirmed in the catalog, childhood photographs of Tami and Ada together from the 1960s have not yet been identified.

## 3. The Artistic Spark & The Pragmatic Choice | בין אמנות למעשיות

From a young age, Tami was an avid lover of visual art with dreams of practicing it one day, yet coming of age in the 1970s she made a deliberate decision to secure financial independence through science and technology first:

> ״כשהלכתי ללמוד מדעי המחשב בטכניון המחשבה הייתה להיות עצמאית כלכלית ולעסוק בשעות הפנאי באמנות. ברור שלא היו שעות פנאי.״
> — תמר שפר, בראיון לענבל כהן חמו, [״כשהסביבה הופכת לחומר״, מגזין *פורטפוליו* (1 באפריל 2023)](https://www.prtfl.co.il/archives/179933)[^prtfl-technion]

## 4. The Technion Years & Meeting Gadi | שנות הטכניון ומפגש החיים

Moving to Haifa to pursue her B.Sc. in Computer Science at the **Technion — Israel Institute of Technology**, Tami excelled in an overwhelmingly male academic environment[^prtfl-technion]. It was in a Linear Algebra 1 class at the Technion that she met Haifa native **[Gad "Gadi" Sheaffer](../family/gad-sheaffer.md)** (born 1954), as Gadi recounted in his Hebrew memoir of their post-graduation travels:

> ״במהלכם סיימתי תואר ראשון בטכניון, עבדתי כסטודנט בחברת אינטל וכמהנדס בחברת אלסינט ז״ל, זו שבנתה מכשירי CT. הספקתי גם להכיר את זוגתי שתחייה בשיעור אלגברה אחד, ואפילו להתחתן אתה, בחתונה מצומצמת לקרובי משפחה בלבד, שהתבקשו להביא מתנות במטבע זר בלבד (היה אז קיצוב מט״ח), לטובת הטיול הגדול באירופה שתכננו לעשות עם תום הלימודים של שנינו.״
> — גד שפר, [״׳הטיול הגדול׳ אחרי צבא״, בלוג *דרכי עצים* (8 ביוני 2023)](https://theshingimel.wordpress.com/2023/06/08/2/)[^blog-trip]
