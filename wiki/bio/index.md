## The Arc of a Life | מסלול חיים

The biographical journey of **Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer** spans seven vibrant decades, moving through three distinct yet deeply connected chapters:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    TAMAR SHEAFFER: 70 YEARS (1956–2026)                                 |
+--------------------------+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------+
| 1. ROOTS & EDUCATION     | 2. DEFENSE SYSTEMS & FAMILY         | 3. CONTEMPORARY ART & MATURITY         |
| (1956–1978)              | (1978–2004)                         | (2005–2026)                            |
| - Herzliya childhood     | - Elbit Systems & Rafael engineer   | - HaMidrasha Art School (2005–2009)    |
| - Parents Ahuva & Michael| - Systems architecture & rigor      | - Univ. of Haifa MFA (2009–2011)       |
| - Sister Er'ela bond     | - Outspoken workplace feminism      | - Binyamin Gallery founder (Tel Aviv)  |
| - Technion Computer Sci. | - Raising Guy & Tal in Haifa        | - Paris Cité residency & Bogliasco     |
| - Meeting Gadi Sheaffer  | - Homes: Arlozorov, Einstein, Denya | - Ramat Gan Museum: "בנה ביתך" (2026)  |
+--------------------------+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------+
```

What makes Tami's biography extraordinary is the absence of rupture between these phases. Her transition from high-tech systems engineering to contemporary installation art was not a rejection of her past, but a profound dialectical synthesis: the engineer’s structural discipline, telemetry logic, and spatial precision were turned inside out to expose the vulnerability, precarity, and Sisyphean beauty of human existence.
