## The Arc of a Life | מסלול חיים

The biographical journey of **Tamar "Tami" Sheaffer** spans seven decades, moving through three distinct yet deeply connected chapters[^site-about][^blog-home]:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    TAMAR SHEAFFER: 70 YEARS (1956–2026)                                 |
+--------------------------+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------+
| 1. ROOTS & EDUCATION     | 2. DEFENSE SYSTEMS & FAMILY         | 3. CONTEMPORARY ART & MATURITY         |
| (1956–1978)              | (1978–2009)                         | (2005–2026)                            |
| - Herzliya childhood     | - Elbit Systems & Rafael engineer   | - HaMidrasha Art School (2005–2009)    |
| - Parents Ahuva & Michael| - Systems architecture & rigor      | - Univ. of Haifa MFA (2009–2011)       |
| - Sister Er'ela bond     | - Outspoken workplace feminism      | - Binyamin Gallery member (Tel Aviv)   |
| - Technion Computer Sci. | - Raising Guy & Tal in Haifa        | - Paris Cité residency & Bogliasco     |
| - Meeting Gadi Sheaffer  | - Homes: Arlozorov, Einstein, Denya | - Ramat Gan Museum: "בנה ביתך" (2026)  |
+--------------------------+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------+
```

What makes Tami's biography extraordinary is the dialectical continuity across these phases: the defense systems engineer's command of spatial scale, technical schematics, and structural dependencies was turned inside out to expose human vulnerability, dissolving social categories, and the fragility of domestic shelter[^site-about][^prtfl-bene].
